You can control the player camera with the keyboard, using the keys below:

  • W - Forward
  • S - Backward
  • A - Left
  • D - Right
  • Q - Down
  • E - Up
  • ArrowUp - Look up
  • ArrowDown - Look down
  • ArrowLeft - Look left
  • ArrowRight - Look right

The skyCamera can be controlled by using the camera sliders, and the toggle can be used to switch between the player camera and the skyCamera.

The three cubes in the scene can be selected using the radio buttons, and their position, rotation, and scale can be adjusted using the sliders.
